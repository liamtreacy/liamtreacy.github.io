Places to Bring Kids in Northumberland

A list of spots to bring the kids in Northumberland.

Longframlington playground

Big playground beside pitches, lots of running space. Just down from The Running Fox.

Free parking.

Giant Spoon - Eat for England, Cramlington

A nice muddy-puddle filled path through some fields to get there.

Free parking at nearby pub.

Dunes Park, Seaton Sluice

Modern, big playground by the seaside. Path runs alongside it and you can explore the dunes too.

Paid parking outside nearby pub (via app).

Juliet Oakley Park, Pegswood

Nice playground.

Free parking.

Callerton Lane play area, Ponetland

One of our recent favourites. The playground has plenty for toddlers and older kids. Nearby to the park that goes through the town.

Free parking. Beside lesuire centre.

Plessey Woods Country Park

Playground and park.

Paid parking (first hour free). Toilets. Cafe.

Bolam Park and Lake

Explore the lake by the surrounding path. We enjoyed it but kids were disappointed there was no playground.

Paid parking. Toilets. Cafe.

Metro trip

We got the metro from Monkseaton to Cullercoats. We spent some time at a lovely playground by the seaside. Although a lot of seagulls around made snack time difficult.

Free parking (depending on station).

Angel of the North

Nice to see once. Not a lot there.

Free parking.

Have not been to
