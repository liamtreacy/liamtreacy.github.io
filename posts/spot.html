Spot

Intro

Rules

Goal

The goal is to end up with the lowest possible score. Each card has a value: Ace is worth 1, number cards their value (their "spot"), and face cards are all worth 10.

Setup

One player is the dealer. They shuffle the deck, offer the player to their right the chance to cut, then deal seven cards to each player and eight to the person on their left. With more players, this becomes six and seven.

Gameplay

To start, the dealer discards one card face-up to create the discard pile. The remaining deck sits face-down beside it.

On your turn:

  1. Pick up a card - either the top card from the discard pile, or draw from the deck (try not to show the other players!)
  2. Drop cards (optional) - you may lay down up to 4 cards, either starting a new set or adding to existing ones
  3. Discard - end your turn by placing one card on the discard pile

Dropping cards

A new drop requires at least three linked cards: either a run from the same suit (e.g. 5-6-7 of hearts) or matching faces (e.g. three fours). Once a set is on the table, any player can add to it on their turn - so if someone has dropped three fours, you could add your fourth.

Ending the game

The game ends when either the deck runs out or a player gets rid of all their cards. Everyone then totals up the cards remaining in their hand.
