Spot

Intro

Rules Every game has one dealer (who is a regular player with additional responsibily) and one other player. The dealer shuffles the cards (offers a player to their right the opporunity to cut the deck) and then deals. Seven cards to each player and eight to the person to their left. Or sometimes if a relatively large number of players it changes to seven and six. The key goal of spot is to end up with the lowest possible score. Each card has a score. Ace is worth 1, 2 worth 2 etc and face cards are all 10. A game can be ended in two ways. The stack (later) runs out of cards or a player gets rid of all of their cards. So how can you get rids of cards? Well you can add to dropped cards, or drop your own. A drop requires a minimum of three cards which are linked. So a run of cards from the same suit. Or the same face (3 fours for example). And let's say there are 3 fours dropped by another player. When it's your turn you can add your 3 of spades to it. You can only drop 4 cards during your turn. Start of the game. The player to the right gets rid of one of their card into the face up stack. The next player can opt to take that card or lift a card from the top of the face down deck (try not to show the other players!). They can then drop cards and end their turn by discarding one card into the face up stack.
