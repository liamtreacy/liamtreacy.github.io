Intro

Spot is a great card game from the west of Ireland. I've never heard of it referenced anywhere else and internet results on it are spotty (gettit). So I decided to write up this piece.

Rules

Goal

The goal is to end up with the lowest possible score. Each card has a value: Ace is worth 1, number cards their value (their "spot"), and face cards are all worth 10.

Setup

One player is the dealer. They shuffle the deck, offer the player to their right the chance to cut, then deal seven cards to each player and eight to the person on their left. With more players, this becomes six and seven.

Gameplay

To start, the dealer discards one card face-up to create the "discard pile". I can't remember the name we used for this! The remaining deck sits face-down beside it.

On your turn (in order):

  1. Pick up a card - either the top card from the discard pile, or draw from the deck.
  3. Discard - end your turn by placing one card on the discard pile

Dropping cards

A new drop requires at least three linked cards: either a run from the same suit (e.g. 5-6-7 of hearts) or matching faces (e.g. three fours). Once a set is on the table, any player can add to it on their turn - so if someone has dropped three fours, you could add your fourth.

Ending the game

The game ends when either the deck runs out* or a player gets rid of all their cards. Everyone then totals up the cards remaining in their hand. *If the facedown pile is all gone, you can keep the turn going by picking from the discard pile and returning to it at the end of your turn. An unusual play but legal.

Memories

My parents taught me to play this when I was young. I caught the bug instantly. I was so excited by their stories of playing it at pubs, in big groups and in wider competitions. I would often beg my parents for one more game during Christmas etc. I can look back now and say that playing with three people, and one of whom is in primary school can't have been that exciting or engaging. But it was to me. Working with Dad. Random playing in pubs. Making friends. How long since I've played.

Summary

Great game. Hope it lives on. More than that giving a child a glimpse into your adult life and making them feel all grown up. Glad overall. Hope to play it with my kids.
