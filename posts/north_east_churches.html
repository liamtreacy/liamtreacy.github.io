Catholic Churches in North East England

Last updated: 28th July 2026

A list of Catholic Churches in Northumberland and nearby.

St. Robert of Newminster, Morpeth

A lovely church built in 1849 and located in the heart of Morpeth. It is here that I was married and my children baptised.

Ample parking in the grounds on Sundays, or nearby free parking by the river.
Website

St. Bedes, Bedlington

I love the wide interior layout of this church, reminds me of the one nearest to where I grew up.

A tight and narrow road to the small carpark. Get there early.
Website

St Aidan's, Ashington

The wooden support high above the altar always catches my interest. Very unusual. This church was the first near us to re-open after first covid lockdown and as such is where we went to Sunday Mass during Summer 2020.

Parking to the rear.
Website

St Charles, Gosforth

The stained glass windows to either side of the altar are amazing. This used to be our local church. We attended Midnight Christmas Mass here in 2018.

Parking to the side.
Website
