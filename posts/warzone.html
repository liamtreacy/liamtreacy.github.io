Call of Duty: Warzone

Intro

I've only played two video games in the past decade. My teenage self would be aghast. But thinking on it recently, I've thoroughly enjoyed those two, the acutal situation of their playing by me tells a story and more broadly it's like eating a fine steak once in a while versus a microwave burger daily. In this piece I want to discuss the first of these games, Call Of Duty Warzone. Warzone is a big free to play PVP. So big map, lots of players, differing game style (teams, solo, capture the flag etc.) all built on a COD base. I spent a significant amount of time playing online MW2 in the early 2010s but just fell out of interest in gaming. I tried to get back in with a PS4 purchase in 2017 but... meh. I just couldn't shake the feeling that it was a waste of time in my life then and the best days of it were behind me. So I didn't even follow gaming news. So how did I find Warzone? Well in early 2020 (I know, I know) I saw that Francis Higgins was live streaming one night. He's one of the finest comedic minds of our time and I thoroughly enjoyed his streams at the time, so of course I turned over. And that was my introduction to it all. Verdansk, diving from the plane, the vehicles, the achievements, being able to recognise all the places in Francis's videos and usnerstand his strategies, I loved it all. As the events of that time proceeded, I got more and more into the game. I absolutely loved it. The huge city and surroundings, trying different load outs and the occasional community events/game-changes the devs would unwrap. Like when the stadium roof opened! The thrill of following a team to a cash out point, waiting, knowing they were nervous and cash rich... as soon as they called you ambush and try and steal the lot. Fantastic. There was some supposed story behind it all but even with as many hours as I put into it, I couldn't tell you what it was about. I remember one evening my wife asking "How didn't you hear everyone?!" at the pots-n-pans-NHS-bang-along. Well I was too into calling in an air strike on the dam, that's why. Priorities. All of a sudden Halloween came around. That year people just left bowls of sweets out and notes not to call to the door. Not that it was needed, I barely saw any kids out. But inside well the devs had outdone themselves with a new mode. Monster and normies. Or something like that. The map wa spooky and if you got bit, you became a monster with super jump and run powers. That's my last memory of a big event there. For us things got normal again and then shortly after my daughter was on the way. I once again lost all interest in gaming and had other things to focus on. But it was a wild ride and it really helped me relax that year. I will always associate the intro music (link) with those Spring days in 2020 with its contrast of the worry in the air, uncertainty versus sheer mayhem in big sprawling battles fought from my study. It was a blast.
